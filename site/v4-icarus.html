What lands in the folder

The one file to open first

SUMMARY.md

Company identifiers, the latest year's numbers, the trailing twelve months rolled forward from the 10-Qs, revenue and EPS history, the risk-diff headline, and an index of everything else.

All ten Items, split out

sections/

Business, risk factors, MD&A, legal proceedings, market risk, financial statements — each one its own file, so you read the section instead of the whole document.

The statements, as filed

statements/

Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and equity, taken from the filing itself rather than a vendor's normalisation. Markdown and JSON.

Ten years, with the sourcing shown

trends.md

Margins, free cash flow, ROE, CAGR and share count across a decade — plus a note on which XBRL concepts fed each row and which were tried and not found.

What the whole thing is worth

valuation.md

The share count three ways — cover page, weighted-average diluted, and fully diluted with options and unvested RSUs from the footnote — then market cap, the enterprise value bridge, EBIT, EBITDA and the multiples.

What changed in the risks

risk_diff.md

Risks added, removed and reworded against last year. Every added risk prints beside its closest dropped counterpart with a similarity score, and a wholesale rewrite gets flagged at the top.

How it works

No key, no quota, no cost

Everything comes from SEC EDGAR, bar one keyless quote call. A full extraction is about seventeen requests and fifteen seconds cold, then instant — filings are immutable once accepted, so the cache never expires. The 10-Q figures ride along in the same XBRL call the ten-year history uses, so the roll-forward costs no extra requests. The one exception is the share price, which is a live quote — keyless, uncached, and the only figure here that isn't tagged data out of a filing. It earns the exception: every other number is a fact about a period that already closed, and a market cap is a fact about right now.

No model touches the filing

The tool extracts and structures, nothing else. Reading is yours to do, or your agent's, with the source text sitting right there to check against.

Missing stays missing

Banks have no gross profit line. Berkshire doesn't tag diluted EPS undimensioned. A blank stays blank and the file says what was tried, rather than quietly substituting something close.
